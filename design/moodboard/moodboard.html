Gold budget

One gold owner per screen (here the hero RSVP), plus the active tab, the logo and the focus ring. Purple carries identity: tags, the poll leader, badges and the "+". Cards are one surface step up with a hairline border, with no drop shadows.

02 · Color

Layered night, popcorn gold

Three surface steps give depth without shadows. The ratios on each swatch are WCAG contrast measured against bg / surface / raised.

Brand purple #2E1F4A returns as purpleContainer for tags, selected chips, the poll leader and your own chat bubbles. #6545E0 is used for solid badges and the "+" button. #7C5CFF fails AA in every text pairing, so it is used only for glows. Purple text uses #A48BFF. Muted text was lightened from #6E6880 (3.7:1, failed) to #8F899F (5.1:1 or better everywhere). Text on gold is always #14101F (13:1).

05 · Motion

Calm, purposeful movement

Every demo uses the real motion tokens. Each screen gets one hero motion, and all of it switches off when the OS asks for reduced motion.

List stagger360ms · 40ms step

Feeds and lists rise in as they load. The stagger is capped at 8 items so long lists don't lag.

Press & haptic120ms · scale .97

Every tappable control scales down slightly and fires a light haptic. Press and hold to feel it.

Reaction pop360ms · spring

Likes and reactions overshoot, then settle, and the icon swaps from outline to fill.

Countdown ring600ms · emphasized

Live watch parties and poll deadlines fill smoothly. A confetti burst plays on the winner reveal.

Motion explains

Things move only because they came from somewhere or are going somewhere. Example: a poster grows into the details page.

One hero per screen

Everything else is a quiet fade or rise.

Fast to respond, slow to reveal

Feedback takes 120ms, reveals take 600ms, and nothing blocks input.

Reduced motion

When the OS asks for it, durations drop to 0, nothing staggers, and haptics still fire.

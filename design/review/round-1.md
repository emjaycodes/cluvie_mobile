# Design Review — Round 1

Reviewer: independent senior product designer
Scope: `design/tokens.md`, `design/moodboard/moodboard.html` (source + `refs/`), `design/references/sources.md`, `design/references/ux-patterns.md`, a sample of `design/references/screens/` (Kiosk 09, Community 02, Filmly 03, Dark Cinematic 05, IRL 11), and the current theme, Home screen and nav bar.
Method: every contrast ratio below was recomputed with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula. Translucent layers were alpha-blended onto their real backdrop before measuring.

## Verdict

The direction is sound. Near-black layered surfaces, poster-first heroes, a single warm accent and calm motion suit "refined cinematic". The base text tokens pass: my figures match the stated ones to within 0.05. But the proposal is **not ready to implement**. The Home mock breaks the spec's own core rule: there are about a dozen gold elements, including three gold-gradient buttons with glows, and the annotation claiming otherwise is false. Purple, half of the brand the owner asked to keep, has dropped to an ambient glow plus an 11px "Pinned" label. The actual brand purple `#2E1F4A` does not appear in the mock at all. Accessibility holds only on flat surfaces. Text on posters, on glass, on gold tints and on purple or semantic fills has no guaranteed AA floor, and several cases fail today. The focus indicator token is about 1.5:1. Several tap targets miss the 44pt bar. `surfaceGlass` means one thing in the tokens and another in the mock. The spec also lacks the state and component layer that a full overhaul needs: disabled, error, focus, skeletons, the composer, toasts and sheets. Fix the blocking items below and do round 2. None of them change the direction. They make it buildable and honest.

## What works

- **Base text tokens are correct and well-chosen.** My computations match the spec: `textPrimary` 18.1/17.0/15.7, `textSecondary` 8.1/7.7/7.1 and `textMuted` 5.9/5.6/5.1 on bg/surface/raised. Lightening muted from the failing `#6E6880` was the right call.
- **The `purple` / `purpleText` split is right.** It correctly identifies `#7C5CFF` as failing text (4.30 on surface) and provides `#A48BFF` (6.3–7.3).
- **`onGold #14101F` on gold (13.1:1)** is a strong, simple rule, and the gold CTA itself is legible and on-brand.
- **The motion ladder is sensible.** 120/220/360/600ms, a 40ms stagger capped at 8, ζ≈0.7 spring, "one hero motion per screen", and "motion explains" are good principles.
- **Patterns fit the product.** Social presence (avatar stacks + names), three-state RSVP with counts, club-as-hub tabs, the "+" create sheet, and designed empty/error/skeleton states are well matched to Cluvie's jobs, and the Letterboxd research grounds them.
- **The anti-patterns list is right.** It retires the design-QA toggles shipping on Home, `0.8 × width` buttons and Quicksand.
- **The surface ladder with no shadows** (bg → surface → raised + hairline) is the correct depth model for dark UI.
- **Licensing hygiene.** Third-party references are credited and git-ignored. Fonts are OFL, Phosphor is MIT and the Lottie licences are noted.

# Design Review: Round 2 (token and component code)

Reviewer: independent senior Flutter engineer / design-systems reviewer
Scope: `lib/core/theme/{app_color,app_spacing,app_motion,app_text_styles,app_icons,app_theme}.dart`, `lib/core/theme/widgets/*.dart`, `pubspec.yaml`, `test/cl_components_test.dart`, plus shell and nav impact on the existing tab screens (`git diff 61042bb..HEAD`, 62 files).
Spec: `design/tokens.md` v1.1, with `design/review/round-1.md` and `design/ux-audit.md` §6 P0-5 as context.
Method: I read every file in scope and recomputed the alpha encodings and contrast with the WCAG 2.x formula. I also ran a throwaway widget-test probe, kept outside the repo, that mounts the real `ClBottomNavBar` in a `ShellRoute` at 390×844 with and without a 34dp home indicator. It measures the FAB, SnackBar and bar geometry, nav semantics actions, focus traversal, padded hit areas and `showClDialog`. Every measured result below is from that probe.

## Tooling results

- `flutter analyze --no-fatal-infos`: **15 infos, 0 warnings, 0 errors**, all in `lib/features` (9 `withOpacity` deprecations, 4 `prefer_final_fields`, 1 brace interpolation, 1 naming). None are in `lib/core/theme` or the tests. Note: deprecated `AppColors`/`AppTextStyles`/`AppSpacing` aliases raise **no** diagnostics because `deprecated_member_use_from_same_package` isn't enabled, so the 116 `AppColors.accent` call sites give no migration signal.
- `flutter test`: **34/34 passed** (`cl_components_test.dart` plus the 3 legacy test files).

## Verdict

The token layer is mostly faithful. Colours, type, spacing, radii, motion durations and the spring match tokens.md. The alpha encodings check out: 0xE0 = 87.8%, 0x14 = 7.8%, 0x24 = 14.1%. The "on" colours are wired correctly. Fonts are bundled, `google_fonts` is gone and Phosphor is pinned behind `AppIcons`. Reduced motion is handled well for the mechanisms the library owns. Most P0-5 items from the UX audit are fixed: light defaults, the 0.8× button, the white spinner on gold, `ClAppBar` pop, the `ClAvatar` param, and the snackbar and empty-state slots. **The library is not ready to be the base for the screen restyle.** The floating nav, the core of this commit, has four functional or accessibility defects, all confirmed by measurement:

- TalkBack can't activate any tab or the "+". Their semantics nodes have no tap action.
- The "+" can't be reached by keyboard or switch access.
- The Communities "Create" FAB renders entirely behind the bar.
- Every SnackBar floats 110dp above the bar instead of 12dp.

There are also two library-wide accessibility failures:

- **No visible focus indicator anywhere except text fields.** The theme gives a 1.8:1 gold wash instead of the 2px ring. On `ClPressable`, `ClButton` and `ClCard` that wash, and the 6% pressed overlay, paint *under* the opaque child, so they are invisible.
- **Padded hit areas sit outside the tappable widget.** `ClButton.sm` is really 40dp and `ClChip` 36dp.

There are also these defects:

- `ClButton` uses a `LayoutBuilder`, which makes `showClDialog` throw (`LayoutBuilder does not support returning intrinsic dimensions`).
- Two tokens disagree with tokens.md: `scrimBottom` stops and `glowPurple`.
- The blur contract (one `BackdropGroup`, real blur only on the nav and app bar) isn't implemented.
- Spinners, progress rings and the refresh indicator default to gold, against the gold budget.

All of these are small, local fixes. None change the design. Fix the blocking items, add tests that would have caught them, and run round 3 on the diff only.

## What works

- **Colour tokens match the spec** apart from C8 and C9. Every hex is exact. `surfaceGlass` 0xE0 = 87.8%, `glassFlat`/`border` 0x14 = 7.8%, `borderStrong` 0x24 = 14.1%, `scrimTop` 0xB3 = 70.2%, `brandGlow` 0x59 = 34.9%, and `glowGold` and `float` are exact. `focusRing`, `borderInput` (3.05–3.30:1), `onGold`, `onPurpleBadge` and `onSemantic` are all present and used correctly.
- **No text on `#7C5CFF` anywhere.** `purple` appears only in glows and confetti. `purpleBadge` carries white, and `purpleContainer` carries `purpleText` or `textPrimary`.
- **`textMuted` only appears on flat surfaces**: hint on `surfaceRaised`, the disabled button, the poster fallback, and the `overline` default. Badges and toasts use `textPrimary`/`textSecondary`.
- **Typography is exact** (sizes, line-height ratios, weights, tracking, default colours). Sora 600/700/800 and Jakarta 400/500/600/700 are bundled, `google_fonts` is removed (S10 resolved) and the `TextTheme` mapping is sensible.
- **The `ColorScheme` mapping is complete and correct.** Every M3 widget (dialog, sheet, snackbar, tooltip, menu, segmented button, input) gets a dark, on-token default. This was round-1 S10.
- **Most of the reduced-motion spec is implemented.** `AppMotion.of` is the single gate, and the implicit animations in the nav, chip, `ClPressable` and ring all read it. `ClEntrance` skips `flutter_animate` entirely, shimmer becomes a static block, confetti never fires, `Hero` is wrapped in `HeroMode`, and the page-transition wrapper keys off `disableAnimations`. Haptics still fire.
- **The nav structure meets the spec.** Inactive labels are kept in `textSecondary` (round-1 S1). There are `selected` semantics, a purple "+", and gold only on the active tab and the "+" active ring. Blur sits inside `ClipRRect`. Tab targets are ≈63×68dp. `contentPadding()` builds on `MediaQuery` padding rather than magic numbers, and the three restyled tab screens use it.
- **UX-audit P0-5 is fixed**: no light defaults, full-width or `expand` `ClButton` with a real disabled state, an `onGold` spinner, a `canPop`-safe back button, the `ClAvatar` param, an action slot on `ClSnackbar` and a CTA slot on `ClEmptyState`. Legacy constructors still compile, `isSecondary` still maps, and `ClEmptyState(message:)` still works.
- **Lifecycle hygiene**: `ClConfetti` moves its listener on `didUpdateWidget` and removes it on dispose. `setState` is guarded by `mounted`. `ClPressable` only calls `setState` on change.
- **The `AppIcons` indirection, the pinned `phosphor_icons: 3.0.1` and Fill-when-active** match tokens.md and round-1 S9.
- **`ClStatusBanner`** uses `liveRegion` and keeps its text `textPrimary` on a flat raised surface, with status colour only on the icon and border. That's the correct pattern.
- **Global `materialTapTargetSize: padded` plus 48dp `minimumSize` in every button theme.** `ClIconButton` (44 visual / 48 hit through Material's input padding) and `ClSectionHeader`'s "See all" do meet 48dp.
